# 📊 Retail Sales Performance & Commercial Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to identify sales trends, evaluate product and category performance, measure profitability, and understand regional business performance.

The project uses Excel's data analysis and visualization capabilities to transform raw sales data into interactive dashboards that support business decision-making.

It includes three interactive dashboards covering Time Analysis, Product & Category Analysis, and Regional Analysis.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales performance.
- Track revenue and profit trends over time.
- Calculate Year-over-Year (YoY) sales growth.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate category-wise profitability and profit margins.
- Compare sales and profit performance across regions.
- Understand customer segment contributions to sales.
- Build interactive dashboards for business reporting.

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

### 1. Time Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes sales and profitability trends over time.

**Key Analysis:**

- Monthly Sales Analysis
- Monthly Profit Analysis
- Quarterly Sales Performance
- Yearly Sales Comparison
- Monthly Sales and Profit Trends
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth

**Business Purpose:**

Understand seasonal sales patterns, monitor business growth, compare annual performance, and identify changes in revenue and profitability.

### 2. Product & Category Analysis Dashboard

Evaluates product-level and category-level performance.

**Key Analysis:**

- Sales by Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Subcategory
- Quantity Sold by Product
- Category-wise Sales Contribution
- Profit and Profit Margin by Category

**Business Purpose:**

Identify high-performing products, understand category-wise revenue contribution, evaluate profitability, and identify products contributing significantly to business performance.

### 3. Regional Analysis Dashboard

Evaluates business performance across different geographical regions.

**Key Analysis:**

- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Sales by State
- Regional Sales Contribution
- Monthly Sales Trends by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment and Region

**Business Purpose:**

Compare regional performance, identify strong and weak markets, evaluate geographical profitability, and understand customer segment contributions across regions.

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

The project includes the following performance indicators:

| KPI                       | Description                                                |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue             | Total sales generated                                      |
| Gross Sales               | Sales before discounts                                     |
| Total Discount            | Total discount amount                                      |
| Total Cost                | Total product cost                                         |
| Total Profit              | Revenue minus cost                                         |
| Profit Margin             | Profit as a percentage of sales                            |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average sales value per order                              |
| YoY Growth                | Percentage change in sales compared with the previous year |
